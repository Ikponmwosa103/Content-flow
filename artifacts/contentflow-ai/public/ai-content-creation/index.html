Frequently asked questions

What is AI content creation?

It is the use of AI tools to help draft, refine and organize written marketing content. You remain responsible for reviewing and approving the final material.

Can I create social media captions?

Yes. ContentFlow includes AI-assisted workflows for social content and platform-aware drafts.

Does ContentFlow publish posts automatically?

No. Calendar items are planning records and are not automatically published to social platforms.
