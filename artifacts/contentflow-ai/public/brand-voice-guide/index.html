Frequently asked questions

What is a brand voice?

Brand voice is the consistent personality and style a business uses in its communication.

What should a brand profile include?

Useful details include the audience, industry, tone, content themes, preferred language and claims or phrases to avoid.

Does a brand profile guarantee every draft is on-brand?

No. It provides context, but human review is still important.
