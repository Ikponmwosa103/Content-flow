Frequently asked questions

What is content repurposing?

It is adapting an existing idea or piece of content into new formats or channel-specific drafts.

Which channels can I prepare drafts for?

ContentFlow's repurposing workflow offers Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and email newsletter targets.

Does repurposing post directly to those platforms?

No. It creates drafts for your review and does not automatically publish them.
